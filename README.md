# TETRIS MOBILE
## RETRO GAME
### Gameplay & Touch Controls
Here’s how the game works and how to play it on your phone.

Core loop: Classic Tetris rules — rotate and drop falling blocks to complete horizontal lines. Each cleared line adds to your score and line count, and every 10 lines increases the level, which speeds up the falling pieces.

Touch controls: Use the five on-screen buttons at the bottom for left, rotate, right, soft drop, and hard drop. You can also swipe directly on the board: tap to rotate, swipe left/right to move, and swipe down to hard drop.
Visual feedback: The game includes a ghost piece that shows where the current block will land, plus a next-piece preview, scoreboard, and subtle vibration feedback on hard drops and line clears.

Pause & resume: The pause button or switching away from the page automatically pauses the game, with an overlay to continue or restart.
